## Context

The programme needs a way to track technology interest without confusing interest with approval.

## Proposed Decision

Maintain a Technology Radar with Adopt, Trial, Assess, and Hold rings. A technology can move to Adopt only after evaluation evidence and ADR approval.

## Evidence Required

- Completed technology evaluation artifacts.
- ADRs for architecture-shaping technology choices.
- Review history for radar ring changes.
