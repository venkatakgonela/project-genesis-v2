## Objective

Design the Technology Discovery Programme that will govern all future technology evaluations before Project Genesis V2 implementation begins.

## Scope

In scope:

- Technology Discovery Programme.
- Technology Discovery Process.
- Technology Evaluation Framework.
- ADR lifecycle for technology decisions.
- Technology decision gates.
- Technology Watch process.
- Technology Discovery Backlog.
- Sequenced future work packages for major capability areas.

Out of scope:

- Runtime implementation code.
- Final technology recommendations.
- Deep technology comparison.
- Technology winner selection.
- Architecture redesign.
